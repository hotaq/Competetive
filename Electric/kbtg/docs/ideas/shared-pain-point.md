# Shared Pain Point — Money Runway & Safe-to-Save

Recorded: 2026-09-08

## Focus for now

Keep one shared pain point before developing the two solution ideas further.

**“เห็นยอดเงินในบัญชี แต่ไม่รู้ว่าเงินจริง ๆ ที่ใช้ได้จนถึงเงินเดือนออกเหลือเท่าไหร่”**

First Jobbers can see their account balance, but that balance also includes money needed for rent, installments, and everyday expenses before the next payday. They may therefore struggle to decide how much they can spend now or set aside as savings without needing to withdraw it later.

## Illustrative example

A user has ฿8,000 in their account, ฿4,000 in rent still due, and 12 days until payday. The displayed ฿8,000 is not entirely available for discretionary spending. These figures are hypothetical, not research findings.

## Connection to the existing ideas

- **Money Runway:** helps answer whether the remaining money will last until payday.
- **Safe-to-Save:** helps estimate how much can be set aside after accounting for upcoming expenses and a buffer.

The shared pain point does not yet constitute a decision to merge or implement the two ideas.

## Validation status

This is a working hypothesis to validate with actual First Jobbers. No user-interview evidence or measured prevalence has been established for this specific statement.
